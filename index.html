SocialReact: Group-Aware Listener Reaction Generation for Multi-Party Conversations

Lownish Rai Sookha1, Pawan Kumar1, M. A. Ganaie1, Abhinav Dhall2
1Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, 2Monash University
Paper Code

Abstract

Human face-to-face communication in small groups is shaped by subtle visual responses such as nods, smiles and micro-expressions, that signal understanding and engagement. While prior works on listener facial reaction generation has largely focused on dyadic interactions, real-world conversations often involve multiple participants whose responses are inter-dependent. In this work, we propose SocialReact, a multi-modal framework for group-aware facial reaction generation in a multi-party setting comprising one speaker, two contextual listener and one target listener. A Finite Scalar Quantization (FSQ)-based motion tokenizer discretizes 3D facial motion representation into a compact and expressive latent space. We model interper-sonal influence using a Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Network (ST-GNN) with directed Graph Attention layers, enabling the target listener's reaction to be conditioned on both the speaker and the contextual listeners. A cross-modal Transformer integrates long-range prosodic and visual cues into a unified speaker context. An auto-regressive GRU-based temporal decoder generates temporally coherent, contextually appropriate facial expressions. By explicitly modeling group dynamics, our approach moves beyond dyadic paradigms and contributes towards a more socially aware intelligent system for applications in tele-presence, virtual agents and human-robot interaction.

Approach

Our framework consists of three main components:

  1. Motion Tokenizer - We first encode continuous facial motion into a compact discrete representation using a Finite Scalar Quantization (FSQ)-based motion tokenizer. A CNN-ResNet encoder downsamples the input motion sequence before quantization, while a decoder reconstructs the original facial dynamics from the discrete tokens. This compact representation preserves expressive facial behaviors while reducing the complexity of the generation task.
  2. Speaker Context Encoding - Given the speaker’s audio and facial motion, we extract acoustic representations using a pre-trained audio encoder and combine them with visual features through a cross-modal Transformer. The resulting representation captures complementary speech and facial cues, providing a unified description of the speaker’s behavior.
  1. Group-Aware Reaction Generation - The fused speaker representation, together with the contextual listener’s facial motion, is processed by a Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Network (ST-GNN). The graph explicitly models speaker-listener and listener-listener interactions, while a recurrent decoder generates temporally coherent facial motion for the target listener, producing socially appropriate reactions that evolve with the conversational context.
